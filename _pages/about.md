<div style="font-family: 'Playfair Display', serif; font-weight: 300;font-size: 16px; line-height: 1.7;">
<p>Margarida Truninger is a social scientist whose work lies at the intersection of organizational behavior and psychology. Drawing on training that spans economics, management, and psychometrics, she examines the social and emotional conditions that shape how people lead, belong, and collaborate, and how these ultimately affect human thriving.</p>
<p>She holds a PhD in Management Sciences from ESADE Business School (Ramon Llull University), where she also completed her Master of Research. Her doctoral work examined the role of emotional intelligence competencies relative to general intelligence and learning performance. She has held postdoctoral positions at the Center for Creative Leadership in the United States and NOVA School of Business and Economics in Lisbon, Portugal.</p>
<p>She has published in <i>The Leadership Quarterly</i> and <i>Frontiers in Psychology</i>, and presented at the Academy of Management, SIOP, and the European Academy of Management.</p>
